Incident notes - payments API slow, Oct 26
02:04 deploy 4.12 of payments-api finished
02:14 latency alert fired, p95 4.8s; on-call engineer paged
02:19 on-call joined bridge, saw "connection pool exhausted" errors on pay-api-03
02:25 DB team confirmed 200/200 connections in use, DB CPU normal
02:31 restarted pay-api-03; latency recovered for 9 minutes then climbed again
02:44 incident commander decided to roll back to 4.11
02:52 rollback complete, connections steady at 40, latency normal
03:20 incident closed; about 12% of transfers failed between 02:14 and 02:52
Cause found next day: 4.12 opened a new DB connection per retry and never closed it.
